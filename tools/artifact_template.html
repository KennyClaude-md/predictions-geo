The topline

Individual probabilities are the easy part. What determines how the decade actually feels is how many high-impact events land — and whether they land together.

Systemic stress over the decade

Every fired event contributes its severity to a global stress index and then decays. A decade with three severity-8 events inside eighteen months scores far higher than one where the same three are spread across ten years — which is the point. Simultaneity breaks systems, not the count.

Headline forecasts

Ranked by expected systemic impact — probability by 2036 times severity — rather than by probability alone, because a 12% chance of something that reorders the world outranks a near-certainty that doesn't. The bracket is not the range of outcomes; the event either happens or it doesn't. It is the range of the probability itself across parameter worlds. Monte Carlo noise has been subtracted, so what is left is real disagreement.

Five shapes the decade takes

Paths were clustered on which major events fired and on the shape of their stress trajectory. These are not scenarios written in advance and then assigned probabilities — they are the shapes the simulation actually produced, priced by how much of the path mass fell into each.

Where the correlations are

Pairs whose joint occurrence most exceeds what independence would predict. Lift is P(both) ÷ P(A)·P(B) — a lift of 3 means these two show up together three times more often than chance. This is the part a spreadsheet of independent probabilities cannot produce, and it is where tail risk actually lives.

What to watch

Share of the variance in peak systemic stress attributable to each event firing at all. High-scoring nodes are worth watching because learning how they resolve collapses the most uncertainty about everything else. One caveat: the stress index is built from these same events, so part of any node's score is its own contribution rather than its influence on others. The ranking still combines probability, impact and correlation with the rest of the system into one number — but it is not a pure causal-influence measure.

Where the worldviews disagree

Five parameterisations — raw analyst, audited, outside-view base rates, structural-break inside view, and prediction-market check — converge on most nodes. These are the ones where they don't, and they are exactly the forecasts to hold most loosely.

Full results by domain

Every node in the model, with the resolution criteria each was scored against. Read the criteria before quoting a number — several of these forecasts move by tens of percentage points on the wording of what counts.

Continuous indicators

These evolve on a Gaussian copula driven by each path's own stress index, so the bad tails here are populated by the same paths that fired the bad events — not by independent noise.

What to watch, and when

Dated forcing functions the analysts flagged. These are the model's inputs, not its outputs — the scheduled moments when a hazard gets resolved or reset. More actionable than any single probability on this page, because they are the points at which you get to update.

What this cannot do

The engine is exact. The parameters are informed judgement. Simulation precision does not create forecast accuracy, and the gap between those two things is where every failure of a model like this one lives.
